Esports and competitive

  • Live matches with turn-based hero and operator bans
  • Map pool rotation per game mode
  • Team registration, rosters and scrim scheduling

Game companions

AI and engagement

  • Ask Aura anything with /chat, @mention or a reply
  • Leveling with a rendered rank card image
  • Suggestions with voting and mod approval
  • Automatic stat lookups when someone posts their BattleTag

Community tools

  • Button reaction roles and onboarding
