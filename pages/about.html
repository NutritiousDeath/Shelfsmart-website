The games went on the shelf

ShelfSmart began as an inventory app for store shelves. That app is gone. The name stayed.

How we got here

The retail app had an AI helper called AuraAI. While the app wound down, AuraAI found a second life in Discord, running game tools and moderation for gaming communities. That became the main thing.

Now ShelfSmart Gaming is the shop for everything that grew out of it: the bot, game servers on our own hardware, and the streams and music made alongside them.

Who runs it

One person: NutritiousDeath. Developer of AuraAI, streamer on Twitch and TikTok, and the composer behind Heaven is Overrated.

Being small means you talk to the person who wrote the code, and that we say plainly what's ready and what isn't. It also means capacity is limited, especially for hosting.

Meet Aura

Aura is the cyborg from the FAFO music video by Heaven is Overrated, and the face of the bot. The purple is on purpose: in FAFO it marks the resistance.
