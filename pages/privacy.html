Privacy

What this website and our services collect, and why. Last updated November 20, 2026.

This page covers the ShelfSmart Gaming website. The AuraAI Discord bot has its own privacy policy and terms of service, which apply whenever you use the bot or its dashboard.

The contact form

When you send a message we receive your name, email, optional Discord username, topic and message. The form is delivered by Formspree, which stores submissions on our behalf. We use them only to reply to you and, for the hosting waitlist, to tell you when hosting opens.

Signing in to the dashboard

The dashboard uses Discord sign-in through Supabase. We receive your Discord user ID, username and avatar, and the list of servers you're in so you can pick which one to manage. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card details.

Game server hosting

If you manage a server through AuraAI, we store which Discord account owns it, the Minecraft usernames on its whitelist, the players currently online, and a log of actions taken on it (such as start, stop or whitelist changes) and who took them.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • This site doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies.

Removing your data

Ask through the contact form and we'll delete what we hold about you, except anything we have to keep for payment records.

The old ShelfSmart app

The ShelfSmart retail inventory app has been discontinued. If you used it and have a question about your data, use the contact form.
